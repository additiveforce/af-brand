# AF colour

Values: `af-color-system.json`. Office theme values: `af-office.json`.

## Rules

- Charcoal type on white is the default for every surface. Colour is the exception, so it carries meaning.
- Screen blue is the one accent: links, key figures, the subject of a chart, the active state.
- The pale-blue tint frames data and findings. Charts and tables sit on a white card inside it.
- The six screen colours (blue, red, jade, teal, purple, bronze) are grounds. A ground fills a whole space, such as a
  cover or section opener, and takes only its own pairing from `grounds` in the JSON. Never use a screen colour for a
  detail inside reading content.
- Labels, captions and metadata take the muted text role. The content they name takes the text role.
- Status pairs (better, watch, stopped) are for small campaign reports only, tint behind and ink on top, always with an
  icon and a word.
- No gradients, textures or shadows.
