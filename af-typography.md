# AF typography

Values: `af-typography.json`. Font: [Geist](https://vercel.com/font), free under the SIL Open Font License.

## Rules

- One family, Geist. Hierarchy comes from a few sizes, weights and two tones, not from extra faces or colour.
- Headings, labels and captions are sentence case. A caption under a figure starts with a capital.
- Display type on covers, claims and openers is Geist Black in capitals.
- Emphasis is weight, never italics or underline.
- Body text is 16px (16pt on slides). Nothing goes below 12pt in a deck.
- Where Geist is missing, Aptos is the substitute in Office files and the system sans on the web.
